# Doodle Playground ("Wen Hsiu Starter Pack" Edition) 🌸✂️🎨

An interactive doodle playground fusing the visual style of the "Wen Hsiu starter pack" blister pack & green craft felt with a tactile snap-off plastic model kit runner and dress-up doll assembly stand.

## Visual Inspirations & Origins

- **Wen Hsiu Starter Pack Blister Card Packaging**:
  - Vibrant green craft felt background (`#279655`), bubblegum pink blister pack header card with hanger punch-hole slot, "made in TAIWAN" stamp, and charcoal hand-drawn marker linework (`#1E232A`).
- **Molded Plastic Model Kit Sprue Runner**:
  - Hot-pink plastic runner with gates holding snap-off items: hair bangs, thought box with glowing idea lightbulb and heart, smiling face with blushing cheeks, round glasses, white crop top tee, wide-leg denim jeans with cuffs, Chuck Taylor high-top sneakers with red gingham socks, and coffee dripper.
  - Companion doll dress-up assembly stand where parts snap together into a living companion.

## Mouse Interactions

1. **Velocity Sparkle & Bubble Trails**:
   - Gliding the mouse across the screen creates an ethereal trail of sparkling stars, floating pastel bubbles, and hearts.
   - Fast whipping triggers speed bursts and harmonic chime notes.
2. **Proximity Eye Tracking & Spring Physics**:
   - Desktop critter icons (My Computer monitor, Headphone Bunny, Folder Narwhal, Dino, Chick in trash can) and the assembled doll actively track your mouse pointer with their pupils.
   - Approaching them causes them to squash, stretch, and blush with spring-mass dynamics.
3. **Model Kit Snapping & Assembly**:
   - Hovering over plastic runner parts wobbles the gates with spring tension.
   - Dragging or clicking parts snaps them off the runner with an authentic plastic **SNAP** sound and sprue debris!
   - Drop or click parts into the **Doll Assembly Stand** to dress up your companion.
   - Once assembled, click **Cheer!** to celebrate with hearts and bouncy hops!
4. **DoodlePaint 98 Canvas**:
   - **Rainbow Ribbon**: Liquid oscillating pastel waves follow your cursor smoothly.
   - **Living Ink Pets**: Sketch any doodle stroke — releasing the mouse sprouts blinking googly eyes, rosy cheeks, and little walking legs that wander around the canvas!
   - **Stickers**: Sprays cute micro-doodles across the canvas.
5. **Interactive Widgets**:
   - **World Domination.exe**: Rubbing your cursor across the progress bar accelerates time from "51 years left" to "100% Cuteness Domination!".
   - **Procedural Sound Engine**: Zero-asset sound effects (pops, snaps, boings, chimes, giggles) with an instant top-bar mute toggle.

## How to Run

From the project root:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Visit [http://localhost:3000/prototypes/doodle-playground](http://localhost:3000/prototypes/doodle-playground) or select **Doodle playground** from the homepage list.
